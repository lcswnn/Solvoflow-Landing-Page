Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

What is Solvoflow?

Solvoflow offers the ability to work through anxiety when you need to. A session asks how you are feeling, takes you through a few short exercises, and asks how you are feeling at the end. The exercises are also there to use on their own, with no session and no questions. The app additionally lists crisis and support numbers for the country you pick. This is the official Privacy Policy for the Solvoflow App and this Website, explaining all that the app does with user data.

Lucas Waunn is the creator and operator of the Solvoflow app. He is an independent developer located in the U.S.A. There is no company backing Solvoflow. As such, Lucas Waunn is considered the Controller of the very limited amount of Information (see below) used in the app. For all things related to Privacy please send an e-mail to support@solvoflow.app.

Summary

There is no sign-up required to use the app, nor will you have to log-in to access its features. Your identity as a user will remain anonymous to us. You will never provide us with a name, an email address, or a password. Data is stored anonymously in our database if you choose to do so. This is to know what works and what doesn't within the app. We are always striving to make the app better, and so collecting this anonymous data will help us know what helps with anxiety and what doesn't. Where an exercise gives you something to type, what you write is kept in memory only, for as long as that exercise is open, so you can walk back a step without losing it. It is never written to your device's storage and never sent to us.

No advertisements exist within Solvoflow. No Advertising Identifiers are utilized. Additionally, Solvoflow has implemented no cross-app/website tracking. We do not sell your Personal Data, nor do we utilize it for advertisement purposes.

What Solvoflow collects

Solvoflow has one switch, Share how the sessions go. It is on by default. The first time you open the app — right after the country question, which never leaves your phone — you are shown exactly what it sends, with the switch itself on that panel, so you can turn it off before you go any further. Afterwards it lives in Settings, behind the gear in the top corner of every screen, so you can change your mind at any time. While it is on, the app sends the following and nothing else:

  • A random identifier. It is generated on your device the first time anything is sent, and it is registered with our database as an anonymous sign-in, which is how the database keeps one install’s records apart from another’s. There is no profile behind it (meaning no name, password, or email). It is not your Apple ID, not your device ID, and not an advertising identifier. It does not survive deleting the app: a reinstall is a new number, and we have no way of connecting the two.
  • Basic technical details, alongside that identifier: which platform the app is running on, your operating system version, the app version, when the identifier was created, when the app was last opened, and whether the switch is on.
  • One record per finished session: the two mood ratings (how you felt before or after the session), what kind of anxiety made you open the app, which exercises you did, when the session started and ended, and the app version. The exercises are recorded as internal identifiers, not as any words you wrote or read.

We use that for one purpose: working out which exercises help, so the app can offer better ones. We read it in aggregate, as averages across everybody, rather than as individual records, and nothing in it identifies you. If a session finishes while your phone is offline, the record waits on the device and is sent the next time the app has a connection. At most a dozen wait at a time; past that the oldest are dropped rather than piling up.

To be explicit about what is not collected, ever:

  • No name, email address, phone number, or password. There is nothing to sign up for and no credentials to lose.
  • No location. Not GPS, not Wi-Fi, not an IP-based estimate. Not even the country you pick for crisis numbers, which never leaves your phone.
  • No free text. Nothing you write in an exercise is transmitted, and no human being reads anything you do in the app.
  • No advertising or device identifiers. No IDFA, no Android Advertising ID, no fingerprinting.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, calendar, or Health data. The app never asks for these and never reads them.
  • No recording. Your phone may list Solvoflow as declaring microphone access. That comes from the audio library the app uses to play sound, which declares the permission whether or not an app records. Solvoflow does not record audio, contains no code that could, and nothing from a microphone is stored or sent.
  • No push notifications. The app has no ability to send you any.

What stays on your phone

Separately from anything sent, the app writes a small amount down on the device itself so it behaves sensibly the next time you open it. None of this is transmitted, and all of it is cleared by Reset my data or by deleting the app:

  • Your settings, such as light or dark appearance and reduced motion.
  • The country you picked for crisis numbers, and your answer to the sharing switch.
  • The last few sessions — the two ratings and what you did — which is what lets the app suggest something other than what it suggested last time.
  • The times you recently opened the app: at most eight timestamps, and only those from the last twelve hours, so the app can tell a hard day from a first visit. Nothing on screen currently changes because of it.
  • Anything you have left part-way through in an exercise, so it is still where you left it when you come back.
  • A note that your Plus unlock is held, so the shelf is not locked while the store is being asked.
  • Session records still waiting to be sent, if the sharing switch is on and the phone was offline. A dozen at most.

Anything Solvoflow places outside the app itself shows the same fixed wording to everybody. It reads nothing from your sessions and sends nothing anywhere; tapping it opens the app.

Analytics, crash reporting and third parties

There are no third-party analytics services, no advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting services in the app. The complete list of outside parties involved in Solvoflow is:

  • Supabase hosts the database that receives the session records described above, and issues the random identifier. They process it on our instructions only.
  • Apple distributes the app and processes any purchase. Apple gives us aggregate download, sales and crash statistics that we cannot tie to an individual, on their own terms.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface on this website. Loading this page therefore contacts a Google server, which receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request. This applies to the website only. The app ships its fonts inside the app and contacts nobody for them.

We give your information to nobody else. We would disclose it if the law required it of us, and we would tell you wherever we are permitted to.

Solvoflow also shows crisis and support numbers for the country you pick. Tapping one hands the number to your phone’s dialer or messaging app, and the directory link opens findahelpline.com in your browser. A second, non-urgent sheet — Need something more? — links out to national health services and to therapy, warmline and treatment directories for the country you picked, which also open in your browser. We are not part of those calls, messages or pages, we never learn that you made one, and nothing comes back to us from them. Once you leave the app, the privacy policy of whoever you contacted applies.

Purchases

Solvoflow is free to start, with a one-time paid unlock for additional content. The payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card details, your billing address, or your Apple account.

Restoring your unlock, on this device or a new one, is handled directly by Apple from your Apple ID’s own purchase history. It does not depend on anything we store, and works whether or not data sharing is on.

If data sharing is on at the moment a purchase settles, we additionally keep our own record of it — which product, Apple’s transaction identifier, and the time, filed under the same random identifier described above. This is bookkeeping only: we use it to understand purchases in aggregate, never to decide what is unlocked. If sharing is off at the time of purchase, no such record reaches us. If you turn sharing off afterwards, a record already sent is kept rather than deleted, unlike session records, so our bookkeeping stays accurate — this has no effect on your ability to restore the purchase, since that was never tied to it. Refunds and receipts are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

Solvoflow is rated for ages 12 and up and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from a child under 13. Because there is no sign-up, no name and no contact detail anywhere in the app, we hold nothing that could identify one. In the UK and the European Economic Area the app is intended for people aged 16 and over.


If you believe a child has used the app and you would like the associated record removed, write to us and we will delete it.

Your choices and your rights

The fastest route is inside the app, and it does not involve us at all:

  • The sharing switch, in Settings behind the gear in the top corner of every screen, stops anything further being sent, discards whatever was waiting to be sent, and tells our server to delete the session records already held under your random identifier.
  • Reset my data, in Settings, does all of that and additionally clears everything the app has kept on your phone, meaning every item under What stays on your phone above. That includes this phone’s note that your Plus unlock is held — but the purchase itself lives with your Apple ID, not with us, and the app asks Apple for it again the next time it opens. You do not lose it and you do not have to buy it again.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.

Wherever you live, you may also ask us directly for a copy of what is held about you, for it to be corrected or erased, for it to be given to you in a portable form, or for us to stop or restrict what we do with it. We will not treat you any differently for asking. To find your records we need the random identifier, which we can help you locate. We have no other way of connecting a record to a person, and we will never ask you for identity documents.


If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area: our legal basis for the session data described above is our legitimate interest in understanding, in aggregate and without identifying you, which exercises help — the data is minimal, pseudonymous under a random identifier, never sold or used for advertising, and you can stop it and erase what has already been sent at any time with the switch, which does not change anything the app does for you. Our basis for keeping a purchase record is the same legitimate interest, applied to our own bookkeeping. In both cases you have the right to object, and the right to complain to your data protection authority. Solvoflow is run from the United States, so anything sent is processed there; that transfer rests on the same legitimate interest, and there is nothing in what is sent that identifies you.


If you are in California: the only category of personal information we collect is identifiers, meaning the random number described above, together with the technical and session details listed alongside it. We collect it for the single purpose stated in this policy. We have never sold personal information and we have never shared it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under the CCPA. We do not collect sensitive personal information, and we do not use or disclose any information for a purpose beyond running the app. You have the right to know, delete, correct, and not be discriminated against for asking, and the switch and the reset button exercise those rights immediately.

Retention and deletion

Session records are kept for as long as they are useful for understanding which exercises help, and in any case no longer than 24 months, after which they are deleted. Purchase records are kept while the purchase remains restorable to you. Anything stored on your phone stays there until you clear it or delete the app.

Everything sent to us travels over an encrypted connection and is stored encrypted at rest. Access is restricted at the database level to the identifier that created a record, so one install can never read, change or delete another’s. No system is perfectly secure, but there is very little here to lose: no names, no contact details, and nothing you wrote.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, we will update this page and move the date at the top. If the change is significant, meaning anything that would collect more than what is listed above, we will say so in the app before it takes effect. We will ask you again rather than assuming your earlier answer still stands. Previous versions are available by email on request.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a deletion request: support@solvoflow.app. We answer within 30 days, usually sooner.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

What is Solvoflow?

Solvoflow offers the ability to work through anxiety when you need to. A session asks how you are feeling, takes you through a few short exercises, and asks how you are feeling at the end. The exercises are also there to use on their own, with no session and no questions. The app additionally lists crisis and support numbers for the country you pick. This is the official Privacy Policy for the Solvoflow app and this website.


Lucas Waunn is the creator and operator of the Solvoflow app. He is an independent developer located in the U.S.A. There is no company backing Solvoflow. For anything related to privacy, write to support@solvoflow.app.

Summary

Solvoflow does not collect any user data. The app has no cross app tracking, no ads, no personalized feeds, or any other data collection mechanisms. Everything the app notes down such as your two mood ratings, which exercises you did, your settings, the country you picked, etc., is written to the phone you are holding and stays there until you clear it or delete the app.


Our App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, which is the label an app receives when it collects nothing at all.

What Solvoflow collects

Nothing. To be explicit, none of the following is collected, transmitted, or held by us, ever:

  • No name, email address, phone number, or password.
  • No usage or session data.
  • No analytics or crash reporting.
  • No location.
  • No free text.
  • No advertising or device identifiers.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, calendar, or Health data.
  • No recording.
  • No push notifications.

An earlier test version of Solvoflow offered a switch that could send anonymous session records, so that we could learn which exercises helped. It has been removed in full, along with everything behind it, because collecting nothing is worth more to this app than the report was. Nothing sent under it has been kept.

What stays on your phone

The app writes a small amount down on the device itself so that it behaves sensibly the next time you open it. This is not collection: it never leaves the phone, we cannot see it, and all of it is erased by Reset my data in Settings or by deleting the app. In full, it is:

  • Your last few sessions — when each started, the two mood ratings, and which exercise was last offered. This is what lets the app suggest something that has helped you before, and something other than what it suggested last time.
  • The times you recently opened the app, as a short list of timestamps, so the app can tell a hard day from a first visit and soften what it says.
  • The country you picked for crisis numbers.
  • Your settings — light or dark appearance, reduced motion, and whether to show scores in the games.

Anything you type

One exercise gives you a box to write in. What you write there is held in memory only, for as long as that exercise is open, so that you can step backward without losing it. It is never written to your phone's storage and never transmitted. When you finish the exercise it is gone. The app says so on the screen itself.

Third parties

There are no third-party analytics services, no advertising networks, and no crash-reporting services in the app. The complete list of outside parties involved in Solvoflow is:

  • Apple and Google distribute the app through the App Store and Google Play. They give us aggregate download and crash statistics that we cannot tie to an individual, on their own terms and under their own privacy policies. This is their relationship with you as a customer of their store, not something the app reports.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface on this website. Loading this page therefore contacts a Google server, which receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request. This applies to the website only. The app ships its fonts inside the app and contacts nobody for them.

We give your information to nobody, because we have none to give. If we were ever compelled by law to hand over records, there would be nothing to hand over.

Links out of the app

Solvoflow shows crisis and support numbers for the country you pick. Tapping one hands the number to your phone's dialer or messaging app. A directory link opens findahelpline.com in your browser, and a second, non-urgent sheet links out to national health services and to therapy, warmline and treatment directories for the country you picked. The Settings screen links to this page in the same way.


We are not part of those calls, messages, or pages. We never learn that you made one, nothing about you is attached to the link, and nothing comes back to us from them. Once you leave the app, the privacy policy of whoever you contacted applies.

Purchases

Solvoflow is free, and there is currently nothing to buy inside it. No part of the app asks you for payment, and no payment is processed.


If a paid unlock is offered in a future version, the payment would be handled entirely by Apple or Google. We would never see your card details, your billing address, or your store account. The app would keep no record of a purchase beyond asking the store whether you hold it, and would send nothing to anyone about it. This page will be updated before any such version ships.

Permissions your phone may list

If you inspect Solvoflow's permissions, two entries may look surprising. Both come from libraries the app uses, not from anything the app does:

  • Microphone. This comes from the audio library that plays the app's sounds, which declares the permission whether or not an app records. Solvoflow does not record audio, contains no code that could, and never asks you for microphone access.
  • Internet. Android apps must declare this to open a web link at all, and Solvoflow declares it for exactly that reason. The app itself makes no network requests of any kind. The only thing that reaches the internet is a link you tap, which is handed to your browser.

Solvoflow also offers a home screen widget. It shows the same fixed words to everybody, reads nothing from your sessions, and sends nothing anywhere. Tapping it opens the app.

This website

This website sets no cookies and includes no tracking scripts of any kind. The only third party it contacts is Google Fonts, described above. As with any website, the host that serves these pages receives your IP address in the course of delivering them; we do not use those records for anything, and we do not combine them with anything, because there is nothing to combine them with.

Children

Solvoflow is rated for ages 12 and up and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from a child under 13 — or from anyone else, since we collect nothing at all. There is no sign-up, no name, and no contact detail anywhere in the app, so we hold nothing that could identify a child or anyone else. In the UK and the European Economic Area the app is intended for people aged 16 and over.

Your choices and your rights

Because nothing is collected, there is no request you need to make of us and no waiting on us for an answer. Everything is in your hands, on your phone, immediately:

  • Reset my data, in Settings behind the gear in the top corner of every screen, erases everything the app has kept on your phone — every item under What stays on your phone above. It takes effect at once.
  • Deleting the app removes all of it as well.

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you the right to ask for a copy of your personal information, to have it corrected or erased, to receive it in a portable form, and to object to or restrict its use. Those rights apply to information a company holds about you. We hold none: we operate no server that receives anything from the app, we have never held an account or an identifier for you, and we therefore have nothing to produce, correct, export, or erase. You are welcome to write to us to confirm this, and we will confirm it. We will never ask you for identity documents.


If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area: the app performs no processing of personal data, so no legal basis is required for it and no international transfer takes place. The only processing connected with Solvoflow is your browser's request to Google Fonts when you load this website, which rests on our legitimate interest in serving the page as designed. You have the right to object, and the right to complain to your data protection authority.


If you are in California: we collect no categories of personal information as the CCPA defines them, and no sensitive personal information. We have never sold personal information and have never shared it for cross-context behavioral advertising. There is no personal information for us to disclose for any business purpose, and none is retained. You have the right to know, delete, correct, and not be discriminated against for asking; the reset button in Settings exercises the practical part of those rights instantly, without involving us.

Retention and security

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Anything stored on your phone stays there until you clear it or delete the app, and it is held in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.


No system is perfectly secure, but the strongest guarantee an app can offer is the one Solvoflow makes structurally: there is nothing of yours to leak, because nothing of yours is ever sent.

Changes to this policy

If what the app does ever changes, we will update this page and move the date at the top. If a future version were to collect anything at all — which would be a change to the App Store privacy label as well as to this page — we would say so in the app, in plain words, before it took effect, and we would ask you rather than assuming. Previous versions of this policy are available by email on request.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@solvoflow.app. We answer within 30 days, usually sooner.

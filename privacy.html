Write this Describe what the app writes to local storage, whether it is included in device backups, and what happens to it when the app is deleted.

Purchases

Children

Write this State a minimum age and how it is enforced. This has to agree with the age rating you set in App Store Connect, and it interacts with COPPA and GDPR-K if you accept users under 13 or 16.

Your choices and your rights

Write this Cover access, correction, deletion and portability, and how someone exercises each. If you have EU or UK users, GDPR applies regardless of where you are based; California adds its own requirements.

Retention and deletion

Write this How long anything is kept, and how a person gets it erased. If nothing leaves the device, this section is short — but it still has to be written.

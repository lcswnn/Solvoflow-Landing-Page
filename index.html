How a session goes

Tap Begin and the app takes you through four steps, in order. It never loops you back to a menu.

  1. 1

    Three physiological sighs

    Two breaths in through the nose, then one long breath out. They calm your nervous system before anything else.

  2. 2

    A calming game

    Pick a game for the kind of anxiety you’re feeling. Visuospatial puzzles give your mind somewhere else to be.

  3. 3

    One more exercise

    Finish with one pick from the library: soundscapes, other breathing patterns, somatic movements and more.

  4. 4

    Put it down

    The session ends there. Close the app and come back when you need it again.

Small tools, made for the hard moments.

Everything here is meant to be used in under five minutes: on the bus, between meetings, or at 2am.

  • Guided unwinding

    Short prompts that walk a spiraling thought back down to something you can hold.

  • Breathing that follows you

    Paced breathing that adjusts to how wound up you actually are, not a fixed timer.

  • Games that pull focus

    Calming visuospatial puzzles that redirect your attention when your mind needs somewhere else to be.

  • Nothing that nags

Common questions

Is Solvoflow a replacement for therapy?

No. Solvoflow is not a replacement for therapy or medical treatment. It’s a self-help tool that takes you through breathing exercises, games and other practices to help you relax and ease stress.

Is my data tracked?

No. Solvoflow doesn’t track or log any user data. There’s no account, no login, and no analytics that collect personal information.

Will Solvoflow send me notifications?

No. There are no notifications, no streaks to keep up and no nudges to come back. Solvoflow only opens when you open it.

Can I cancel anytime?

There’s nothing to cancel. Free stays free, and Plus is a single payment rather than a subscription.

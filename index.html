FAQ

Reasonable questions.

Still stuck? Email support@solvoflow.app and a person will answer.

Is Solvoflow therapy?

No. Solvoflow is a self-help tool for getting through a difficult moment, not treatment and not a replacement for a therapist or doctor. If you are in crisis, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line.

What happens to what I write?

Your notes are stored on your device and are not uploaded. If you turn on sync with Plus, they are encrypted before they leave your phone. We cannot read them either way.

How long does a session take?

About four minutes for a typical session. Breathing on its own takes closer to ninety seconds, and you can stop at any point without losing anything.

Will it pester me with notifications?

No. There are no streaks, no reminders and no re-engagement nudges. Solvoflow only opens when you open it.

Can I cancel anytime?

There is nothing to cancel. Free stays free, and Plus is a single payment rather than a subscription.
